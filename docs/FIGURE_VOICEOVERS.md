# Figure voiceovers

Prepared scripts for 21 figures and 38 selectable demonstrations, in menu order.
Each heading identifies the figure and selected variant; only the paragraph below
it is spoken. Clockwise and counterclockwise refer to the overhead view. Screen
left/right describe floor positions; hand names are anatomical. CW means clockwise;
CCW means counterclockwise. The gentleman is dancer A; the lady is dancer B.

Delivery: a composed ballroom instructor speaking British English in Received
Pronunciation. Formal, clear and unhurried, with restrained warmth. Use natural
sentence pauses. These are introductions, not cues timed to the six-second loops.
These describe game movements, not formal ballroom footwork. Mirrored waltz-inspired
variants are game adaptations. Recording status and the continuation list live in
[VOICEOVER_RECORDINGS.md](VOICEOVER_RECORDINGS.md); generation instructions live in
[VOICEOVER_GENERATION.md](VOICEOVER_GENERATION.md).

## Travelling turn - Clockwise

The partners join both hands and draw the gentleman's left arm and the lady's right arm halfway inward. Together, they travel across the floor through a clockwise half-turn, then extend their arms to finish.

## Travelling turn - Counterclockwise

The partners join both hands and draw the gentleman's right arm and the lady's left arm halfway inward. Together, they travel across the floor through a counterclockwise half-turn, then extend their arms to finish.

## Turn in place - Clockwise

The partners maintain a two-hand hold and circle clockwise around a shared centre. They complete one full revolution, keeping the centre of their movement in the same place.

## Turn in place - Counterclockwise

The partners maintain a two-hand hold and circle counterclockwise around a shared centre. They complete one full revolution, keeping the centre of their movement in the same place.

## Alternating sides

The partners keep both hands joined. They draw one side of the hold halfway inward, extend it as the other side closes, then reverse the movement. Both sides open again to finish.

## Open and close

The partners travel together with both hands joined. They bend both arms to draw closer, then extend them to open the hold. This closing and opening is repeated as they continue across the floor.

## Join behind the back

The partners begin apart, with their backs towards one another. Both sweep their arms behind them and move closer until their hands meet. They join both hands behind their backs and hold the position.

## Connected turn - Clockwise

The gentleman keeps his left hand joined to the lady's right and remains in place. The lady draws both arms inward and completes a full clockwise turn. She then extends her joined arm once more.

## Connected turn - Counterclockwise

The gentleman keeps his left hand joined to the lady's right and remains in place. The lady draws both arms inward and completes a full counterclockwise turn. She then extends her joined arm once more.

## Release, turn, catch - Left to right

The gentleman stays in place, with his left hand joined to the lady's right. The lady begins to his left on the screen. She turns clockwise, releases the hold and passes across his front. Completing a full turn on the other side, she joins her left hand to his right.

## Release, turn, catch - Right to left

The gentleman stays in place, with his right hand joined to the lady's left. The lady begins to his right on the screen. She turns counterclockwise, releases the hold and passes across his front. Completing a full turn on the other side, she joins her right hand to his left.

## Open out and return - Gentleman left / lady right

## Open out and return - Gentleman right / lady left

## Progressive twinkles - Start left

The partners advance along a zigzag path. The lady first crosses from left to right on the screen, passing in front of the gentleman, then crosses back. At each crossing, they release the handhold and join the opposite pair of hands.

## Progressive twinkles - Start right

## Open impetus - Clockwise

The partners begin facing one another, with the gentleman's left hand joined to the lady's right. They turn clockwise around the gentleman, then open into a V-shaped position. From here, they travel together in a shared direction.

## Open impetus - Counterclockwise

## Wing - Right to left

The partners begin with a single handhold. The lady follows a semicircle across the gentleman's front, passing from right to left on the screen. They release and change hands during the crossing, then settle facing one another.

## Wing - Left to right

The partners begin with a single handhold. The lady follows a semicircle across the gentleman's front, passing from left to right on the screen. They release and change hands during the crossing, then settle facing one another.

## Weave from promenade - Clockwise

## Away and together - Gentleman left / lady right

## Solo turns and rejoin - Gentleman CW / lady CCW

## Synchronous spins - Clockwise

## Synchronous spins - Counterclockwise

## Two planets - Clockwise

The partners circle clockwise around a shared midpoint without joining hands. They remain on opposite sides of the circle, facing one another throughout. One complete orbit brings them back to their starting places.

## Two planets - Counterclockwise

The partners circle counterclockwise around a shared midpoint without joining hands. They remain on opposite sides of the circle, facing one another throughout. One complete orbit brings them back to their starting places.

## Spinning planets - Clockwise

## Spinning planets - Counterclockwise

The partners orbit a shared midpoint counterclockwise without joining hands. As they travel around the circle, each also spins counterclockwise. Each completes two full body turns during a single orbit.

## Travelling turns - Clockwise

The partners move across the floor on parallel paths, without joining hands. As they travel, each completes one full clockwise turn. They progress together, keeping space between their separate paths.

## Travelling turns - Counterclockwise

## Mirror paths

The partners follow mirrored curves without joining hands. They approach one another, curve apart, then draw near again. Throughout the movement, they continue to face each other.
