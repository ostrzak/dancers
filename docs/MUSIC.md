# Music

The title screen plays Chopin's Waltz in A minor. Choose **Music** on the
title screen, or the **Music** tab while paused, to select the dance track:
Chopin, Devonshire Waltz Moderato (default), The Blue Danube, Gymnopédie No. 1,
or Silence. The selection starts on entering the dance; changes during a
dance take effect immediately, including while paused.

Music continues through pause menus. Volume applies to both title and dance
music; set it to zero to mute both. Selection and volume are saved in
`user://coop_music.cfg`. Music does not control movement or demonstration speed.
Complete recordings repeat with short fades; track changes fade out and in.

Downloaded files were renamed, with original audio bytes preserved, under
`assets/music/`. See `assets/music/CREDITS.txt` for recording-specific sources
and licenses. These credits are also available in the game's Music tab.
